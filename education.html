Understanding HS Code
Trade Basics · 2h 30m · Module overview & quick quiz

What is an HS Code?

The Harmonized System (HS) is an internationally standardized system of names and numbers to classify traded products. Developed by the World Customs Organization (WCO), it's used by more than 200 countries to classify over 98% of global merchandise trade.

Structure of an HS Code (6 digits)

0901.21
09 = Chapter (Coffee, tea, maté and spices)
0901 = Heading (Coffee, whether or not roasted or decaffeinated)
0901.21 = Subheading (Coffee, roasted, not decaffeinated)

Example: Arabica Coffee Export

For specialty Arabica Coffee exported from Indonesia to Japan, the correct HS Code is 0901.21. Some countries add 2 or 4 more digits for tariff statistical purposes (e.g. 0901.21.00.00 in Japan).

💡 Tips: Always verify the HS Code with your destination country's customs tariff schedule — small classification differences can change tariffs by 5–15%.

SPS Requirements for Food Products
Compliance · 1h 45m · Module overview & quick quiz

What is SPS?

Sanitary and Phytosanitary (SPS) measures are the laws, regulations, standards and procedures that governments use to protect human, animal and plant life from pests, diseases and food safety risks in international trade.

The WTO SPS Agreement

The WTO Agreement on the Application of Sanitary and Phytosanitary Measures (1995) sets out the basic rules for food safety and animal/plant health standards. It allows countries to set their own SPS standards but they must be science-based, applied only to the extent necessary, and not arbitrarily discriminatory.

Example: Arabica Coffee to Japan

Japan SPS requirements for green/roasted coffee:
  • Phytosanitary Certificate (issued by Indonesian quarantine authority)
  • Maximum residue limits (MRLs) on pesticides — checked against Japan's Positive List
  • Aflatoxin testing (for some categories) — max 10 ppb
  • Food Sanitation Act compliance labelling in Japanese
  • Manufacturing/HACCP-equivalent process documentation
✅ Pre-export checklist: Phytosanitary cert · MRL lab test · Aflatoxin test · Japan-language label · HACCP docs.

1. Which WTO agreement covers SPS measures?

✓ B. SPS Agreement
The WTO SPS Agreement (1995) governs food safety and plant/animal health standards.
