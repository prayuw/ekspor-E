What you need to file

Every export shipment requires a PEB (Pemberitahuan Ekspor Barang) filed through the CEISA system. For agro products, a LS (Persetujuan Ekspor) may also be required for regulated commodities like cocoa, palm oil, and certain spices.

How Expor-E connects

Expor-E auto-generates the PEB draft from your commercial invoice and packing list, then submits it to CEISA via integration — reducing declaration time by up to 70%.
